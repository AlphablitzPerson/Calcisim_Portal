 CALCism — Five-Stage Reading Pathway

The Temple of Knowledge · Sora Izumi Dao (空 泉 道)

> No theory is sacred enough to override reality.

This is a curated sequence, not a compulsory canon. Reading levels are editorial estimates; verify editions and lawful access.

 Stage 1 — Foundations: Critical Thinking and Social Inquiry

Purpose: Build habits of evidence-based inquiry before adopting a political framework.

   Book  Author  Level  Year  Why read it here 
---:------------:---
 1  How to Lie with Statistics  Darrell Huff  Beginner  1954  Spot misleading statistical presentations. 
 2  The Demon-Haunted World  Carl Sagan  Beginner  1995  Practise scepticism, evidence and intellectual humility. 
 3  Calling Bullshit  Carl T. Bergstrom and Jevin D. West  Beginner  2020  Evaluate data claims and information manipulation. 
 4  The Art of Statistics  David Spiegelhalter  Beginner  2019  Reason about uncertainty and evidence. 
 5  The Sociological Imagination  C. Wright Mills  Beginner  1959  Connect individual experience to social structures. 
 6  Fragments of an Anarchist Anthropology  David Graeber  Beginner  2004  Explore political possibilities beyond state-centred assumptions. 
 7  The Dawn of Everything  David Graeber and David Wengrow  Intermediate  2021  Question simple narratives about inequality and states. 
 8  The Elements of Moral Philosophy  James Rachels and Stuart Rachels  Beginner  1986  Map ethical arguments before evaluating them. 
 9  Mutual Aid: A Factor of Evolution  Peter Kropotkin  Beginner  1902  Introduce cooperation as a social and evolutionary theme. 
 10  The Conquest of Bread  Peter Kropotkin  Beginner  1892  Begin with a foundational statement of anarchist communism. 

Stage checkpoint: Summarise the strongest argument, the best counterargument, and what evidence could change your view.

 Stage 2 — Power, Class, Economy and Inequality

Purpose: Study institutions, economic arrangements, social identities and historical power. Compare traditions rather than treating one author as final authority.

   Book  Author  Level  Year  Why read it here 
---:------------:---
 1  The Communist Manifesto  Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels  Beginner  1848  A short historical statement of class struggle and communist politics. 
 2  Socialism: Utopian and Scientific  Friedrich Engels  Beginner  1880  A classic introduction to Engels's socialist theory. 
 3  The Two Souls of Socialism  Hal Draper  Intermediate  1966  Distinguish emancipatory socialism from socialism imposed from above. 
 4  The Wealth of Nations  Adam Smith  Advanced  1776  Read a foundational account of markets and political economy critically. 
 5  Doughnut Economics  Kate Raworth  Beginner  2017  Consider social needs within ecological limits. 
 6  Governing the Commons  Elinor Ostrom  Advanced  1990  Examine evidence about communities managing shared resources. 
 7  Seeing Like a State  James C. Scott  Intermediate  1998  Study how state simplification can misread complex societies. 
 8  A People's History of the World  Chris Harman  Intermediate  1999  A broad, explicitly interpretive history from below. 
 9  The Black Jacobins  C. L. R. James  Intermediate  1938  Study revolution, slavery, colonialism and Haitian independence. 
 10  The Wretched of the Earth  Frantz Fanon  Advanced  1961  Analyse colonial domination and decolonisation. 
 11  Feminism Is for Everybody  bell hooks  Beginner  2000  An accessible introduction to feminism and structural power. 
 12  Transgender History  Susan Stryker  Intermediate  2008  Explore histories of transgender movements and communities. 

Stage checkpoint: Summarise the strongest argument, the best counterargument, and what evidence could change your view.

 Stage 3 — Socialism, Communism, Anarchism and Workers’ Self-Management

Purpose: Compare social ownership traditions, with particular attention to bottom-up self-management versus centralised authority.

   Book  Author  Level  Year  Why read it here 
---:------------:---
 1  Principles of Communism  Friedrich Engels  Beginner  1847  A concise question-and-answer introduction to communism. 
 2  The Civil War in France  Karl Marx  Intermediate  1871  Marx’s analysis of the Paris Commune. 
 3  Critique of the Gotha Programme  Karl Marx  Intermediate  1875  A short influential discussion of post-capitalist distribution. 
 4  Communism and Anarchy  Peter Kropotkin  Beginner  1901  Compare communist aims with anti-authoritarian organisation. 
 5  The ABC of Communist Anarchism  Alexander Berkman  Beginner  1929  A plain-language introduction to anarchist communism. 
 6  Anarchy  Errico Malatesta  Beginner  1891  A compact introduction to anarchist ideas and social change. 
 7  Anarchism and Other Essays  Emma Goldman  Beginner  1910  Essays on freedom, authority, education and social life. 
 8  What Is Property?  Pierre-Joseph Proudhon  Intermediate  1840  A historically influential critique of property relations; read with context. 
 9  The Federative Principle  Pierre-Joseph Proudhon  Intermediate  1863  Explore federation and decentralised political organisation. 
 10  Anarcho-Syndicalism  Rudolf Rocker  Intermediate  1938  Trade unions, direct action and worker self-management. 
 11  Workers' Councils  Anton Pannekoek  Advanced  1946  An advanced account of council-based self-management. 
 12  The Russian Revolution  Rosa Luxemburg  Intermediate  1922  A revolutionary socialist critique of authoritarian tendencies. 

Stage checkpoint: Summarise the strongest argument, the best counterargument, and what evidence could change your view.

 Stage 4 — Communalism, Ecology, Feminism and Institutions

Purpose: Move from political theory towards institutions, ecological limits, care, federation and practical collective decision-making.

   Book  Author  Level  Year  Why read it here 
---:------------:---
 1  Post-Scarcity Anarchism  Murray Bookchin  Intermediate  1971  Connect technology, ecology and anti-authoritarian organisation. 
 2  The Next Revolution  Murray Bookchin  Intermediate  2015  Introduce libertarian municipalism and communalism. 
 3  The Ecology of Freedom  Murray Bookchin  Advanced  1982  A demanding study of hierarchy, domination and ecological politics. 
 4  The Cooperative Commonwealth  Laurence Gronlund  Intermediate  1884  A historical proposal for cooperative social organisation. 
 5  The People's Republic of Walmart  Leigh Phillips and Michal Rozworski  Beginner  2019  Discuss planning and coordination at large scale. 
 6  Another Now  Yanis Varoufakis  Intermediate  2020  Explore a contemporary fictional alternative to capitalist institutions. 
 7  The Second Sex  Simone de Beauvoir  Advanced  1949  A foundational, advanced work in feminist philosophy. 
 8  The Making of the English Working Class  E. P. Thompson  Advanced  1963  Study class as a historical process and collective formation. 
 9  The Art of Not Being Governed  James C. Scott  Advanced  2009  Explore state avoidance and Southeast Asian histories. 
 10  The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life  Erving Goffman  Intermediate  1956  Understand interaction, social roles and everyday institutions. 
 11  The Social Construction of Reality  Peter L. Berger and Thomas Luckmann  Advanced  1966  An advanced account of how social knowledge is produced. 
 12  The General Idea of the Revolution in the Nineteenth Century  Pierre-Joseph Proudhon  Advanced  1851  A challenging extension on social and political transformation. 

Stage checkpoint: Summarise the strongest argument, the best counterargument, and what evidence could change your view.

 Stage 5 — CALCism Synthesis: Materialism, Religion, Philosophy and Further Study

Purpose: Bring the strands together without treating CALCism or any theory as immune to criticism. Compare naturalistic inquiry, moral philosophy, religious traditions and their critics.

   Book  Author  Level  Year  Why read it here 
---:------------:---
 1  The Structure of Scientific Revolutions  Thomas S. Kuhn  Intermediate  1962  Examine scientific change, paradigms and the history of science. 
 2  Cosmos  Carl Sagan  Beginner  1980  Place human history and inquiry within a scientific cosmic perspective. 
 3  The Origin of the Family, Private Property and the State  Friedrich Engels  Advanced  1884  Read a historically influential theory alongside later scholarship. 
 4  Capital, Volume I  Karl Marx  Advanced  1867  Advanced political economy; attempt after introductory economics. 
 5  The German Ideology  Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels  Advanced  1846  Advanced materialist social theory; read with historical context. 
 6  God and the State  Mikhail Bakunin  Beginner  1882  An anti-authoritarian critique of religious and political authority. 
 7  Why Buddhism Is True  Robert Wright  Intermediate  2017  A contemporary psychological interpretation, not a neutral survey of Buddhism. 
 8  Buddhism Without Beliefs  Stephen Batchelor  Beginner  1997  A modern secular interpretation to compare with traditional and scholarly sources. 
 9  Buddhism: A Very Short Introduction  Damien Keown  Beginner  1996  A compact scholarly orientation to Buddhist traditions. 
 10  Tao Te Ching  Laozi  Beginner  c. 400 BCE  Read a translation alongside scholarship; interpretations vary. 
 11  The Righteous Mind  Jonathan Haidt  Intermediate  2012  Consider psychological explanations of moral and political disagreement. 

Stage checkpoint: Summarise the strongest argument, the best counterargument, and what evidence could change your view.

 After Stage 5

Continue through specialist paths in the full catalogue. Compare primary texts, insider accounts, ex-member testimony, investigative reporting and scholarly work. A source can be valuable without being neutral.
