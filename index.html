TRANSPARENCY
How CAPSTE works
Methodological limits are part of the product, not fine print.
Elite mode
Elite adds 30 diagnostic follow-ups to the Advanced question set. It improves discrimination between related positions but cannot identify a branch with certainty when the underlying profile evidence is missing.
Emoji labels
Emoji tags are optional visual shorthand only. Their definitions are shown in the library and they are never treated as evidence or used as the sole indicator of a result.
Matching model
For numeric answers, similarity uses 1 − |answer − profile| / 4 on a −2 to +2 scale. The profile vector is a curated heuristic prior. Missing, unsure and not-applicable answers are excluded. “Mixed / depends” remains a substantive answer.
What scores cannot say
Scores do not determine truth, morality, the worth of adherents or how an individual behaves. Unknown branch positions must not be guessed.
Evidence standards
Prefer primary sources for textual claims, specialist scholarship for context, transparent surveys for demographics, and documented testimony plus corroboration for lived practice. Record uncertainty and counterevidence.
Privacy
Answers use browser local storage. No analytics, account, remote submission or AI upload is implemented. Exported answers may reveal sensitive beliefs. Legal review is needed before adding cloud or email features.
Accessibility
Semantic headings, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, responsive layout, reduced-motion support and non-colour-only labels are included. No formal WCAG audit has been completed.
Next research priorities
Deep branch-level work for Islam, Hindu traditions, Pagan reconstructionisms, Indigenous community-specific contexts, African diasporic traditions and metaethics. Each requires dated sources, counterevidence and review notes.
