European copyright safeguards: The Temple of Knowledge links to legitimate sources rather than hosting book files. Across the EU, the general term for literary works is the author's life plus 70 years, but exceptions and national rules apply. A book being public domain in the United States does not automatically mean it is public domain in Europe. Translations, introductions, annotations, illustrations, scholarly editions and modern covers may have separate rights. We will label a title “verified public domain” only after checking the relevant edition and jurisdiction; otherwise we will say “licence/status to check”. How links work: Startpage is the default search engine for free-edition discovery; you can switch to Ecosia, DuckDuckGo or Mojeek. Google and Bing are hidden by default and can be unlocked after a warning. They are proprietary services; use them at your own risk. These search services are not all fully FOSS. A search result or catalogue record does not guarantee free access or permission to redistribute. Amazon links are restricted to the physical Books catalogue, not Kindle. Always check the edition, availability, licence and national copyright status.
